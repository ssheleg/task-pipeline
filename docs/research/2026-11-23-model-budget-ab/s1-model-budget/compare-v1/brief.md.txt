# Brief: model and token budget by role in task-pipeline

Size: M. Repository: task-pipeline (a skill: doctrine in Markdown plus standard-library Python scripts that agents run).

## The owner's order (2026-11-23, relayed by the Fabric CEO; his words, translated)

Fable 5.2 through the API (paid by tokens) is used only for the key thinking: decisions, the meaning and the key
features of a spec, analysis and planning that decide something; there it is used without stinting. The spec's text
itself is written by writer agents on Opus 5.5 subscriptions from Fable's meanings. Smart but routine work runs on Fable
in a subscription session; the main work on Opus 5.5 subscriptions; code and mathematics specialist agents on
subscriptions. Decision validators (GLM, Kimi, Grok, Gemini, Astra) answer through tokens, one request each. The usage of
every token route is tracked.

What to do: (1) review where the spec loop, the consilium script, research and other stages call which model and on
which route; (2) bring them to the rule: split spec definition into meanings (Fable by API) and text (Opus writer on
the subscription); consilium validators one call each by tokens, Fable gives the final verdict; (3) spend accounting:
every token call written with route, model, cost and task; a day and month summary visible to the owner (through
Observatory or OpenRouter); a warning when the estate approaches the development-mode ceiling of USD 5,000 a month;
(4) do not lose quality.

## Facts

- `scripts/consilium.py` (v1.113.0) runs a blind panel of six vendors through OpenRouter and a final judge
  (Fable 5.2); its ledger records cost per call. Measured on 2026-11-23: a call through our own OpenAI key (BYOK)
  returns `usage.cost = 0` with the real cost in `usage.cost_details.upstream_inference_cost`; a `--judge-only`
  rerun zeroes the panel's cost in the round's ledger.
- `scripts/spec_protocol.py` has a `research validate` verb that calls one validator model; it records usage but
  no cost.
- The previous rule (2026-11-22) said the spec's author is the session's own model and a paid author runs only on the
  owner's request; this order replaces it.
- Observatory (the estate's inventory tool) already measures every project's monthly spend at the providers
  (`tools/spend.py project --json`, field `totalUsd`).
- The repository's Markdown is under a word-count ratchet: doctrine may not grow in total words.


## How to judge the two candidates (Option X and Option Y)

Both were written for this brief. Pick the spec a zero-context implementer would build the RIGHT thing from with
the fewest questions and the least rework. Criteria, in order: (1) correct and complete against the brief and its
facts (nothing the owner asked is missing or contradicted); (2) buildable: exact contracts, verifiable
requirements, edge cases; (3) decisions made with reasons, no tactical question pushed to the owner; (4) no
invention beyond the brief that the facts do not support; (5) concise for what it must carry. Length is not a merit.
In `score` give each option 0-10.
